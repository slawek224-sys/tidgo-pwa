Getting started with MTD

Making Tax Digital starts with keeping business records digital. For most busy self-employed people, landlords and CIS workers, the first step is simple: keep income, expenses and proof in one tidy place before quarterly deadline pressure arrives.

TidGo helps with simple digital record preparation. It does not submit tax returns to HMRC and does not replace professional tax or accounting advice.
