MTD for sole traders

For sole traders, MTD is easier to face when receipts, dates, amounts, categories and income proof are already organised. TidGo helps with the record-keeping layer before your accountant or MTD-compatible software handles the formal submission workflow.

TidGo helps with simple digital record preparation. It does not submit tax returns to HMRC and does not replace professional tax or accounting advice.
