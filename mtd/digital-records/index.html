Digital records for MTD

Digital records are the foundation of MTD. A useful record is more than a random photo in the camera roll: it needs a date, amount, category and supporting proof you can find again. TidGo keeps the proof beside the record.

TidGo helps with simple digital record preparation. It does not submit tax returns to HMRC and does not replace professional tax or accounting advice.
