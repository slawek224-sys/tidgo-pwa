Who TidGo is for

TidGo is for people who need simple records, not a full accounting suite: self-employed workers, sole traders, landlords, CIS workers, side-income earners and accountants helping clients stay organised.

  • Receipt photos, CIS documents, income proof and summaries in one place.
  • WhatsApp and email intake for easier document collection.
  • Read-only accountant portal and PDF/CSV packs.
  • Free during early access; planned simple pricing from £4.99/month after launch.

TidGo helps with simple Making Tax Digital record preparation. It does not submit tax returns to HMRC and does not replace professional tax or accounting advice.
