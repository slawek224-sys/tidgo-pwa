Who TidGo is for

TidGo is for people who need simple records, not a full accounting suite: self-employed workers, landlords, side-income earners and accountants helping clients stay organised.

  • Receipt photos, income proof and summaries in one place.
  • WhatsApp and email intake for easier document collection.
  • Read-only accountant portal and PDF/CSV packs.

TidGo is simple record-keeping software. It does not submit tax returns to HMRC and does not replace professional tax or accounting advice.
