From a carrier bag in the van to a simple tool - the TidGo story

Why a van driver built a receipt app for the UK self-employed. TidGo helps collect receipts by WhatsApp, forward CIS payslips, and hand tidy records to your accountant.

  • Built from first-hand self-employed paperwork pain.
  • Focused on receipt photos, income proof, summaries and accountant handoff.
  • TidGo does not submit to HMRC and does not replace your accountant.
