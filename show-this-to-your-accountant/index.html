TidGo for accountants

TidGo gives your client a simple way to collect receipts, income proof and CIS documents during the year. You get free read-only access, flagged items and clean PDF/CSV packs when it is time to work.

What TidGo collects. What you still do.

TidGo collects what has a receipt, payslip or document - captured as it happens through WhatsApp, email or the app. That covers most of the day-to-day: fuel, materials, CIS payslips, invoices and income proof.

What TidGo does not replace - and does not try to:

  • Bank statement analysis for direct debits, subscriptions and expenses without a separate receipt.
  • Proportion calculations for mixed-use expenses such as phone, car and home office.
  • Relief claims, capital allowances, CGT and anything requiring professional judgement.
  • The final annual declaration - that stays with you.

Your client arrives with the receipts and payslips already organised. You collect the rest from bank statements at year end, as always. The difference is that the first part arrives tidy instead of in a bag.

We actively recommend that every TidGo user works with an accountant at least once a year. Not because the software is incomplete, but because tax optimisation requires a professional who can see the full picture.

What accountants can see

  • Receipt photos and income proof.
  • Monthly, calendar-quarter and UK tax-quarter summaries.
  • CIS payslips and forwarded documents with originals attached.
  • PDF and CSV export packs.

What TidGo does not do

TidGo does not submit to HMRC, replace accountants, do bookkeeping, VAT, payroll, limited company accounts or tax advice.
