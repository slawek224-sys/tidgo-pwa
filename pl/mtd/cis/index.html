TTD for CIS workers

CIS workers often collect proof from the van, the site, WhatsApp, email and screenshots. TidGo is built as a simple front door for receipts, tools, fuel, materials and remittance evidence before the records go to an accountant.

TidGo helps with simple digital record preparation. It does not submit tax returns to HTRC and does not replace professional tax or accounting advice.
