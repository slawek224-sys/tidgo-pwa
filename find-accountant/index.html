Find an accountant who understands tidy digital records

TidGo is building a small early access introduction list for self-employed people, landlords, CIS workers, accountants and bookkeepers.

  • Users can tell TidGo what kind of accounting help they need.
  • Accountants and bookkeepers can ask to join the early TidGo list.
  • No public ranking yet; introductions are handled manually during early access.

TidGo may help with introductions, but it is not an accountant, tax adviser or broker. You choose and check any accountant yourself.
