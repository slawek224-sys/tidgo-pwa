TidGo

Snap it on WhatsApp. TidGo keeps it tidy for MTD.

Send receipt photos, forward CIS payslips, or type a quick income amount. TidGo keeps MTD records tidy, flags what needs checking, and prepares a clean handoff for your accountant.

  • Receipt photos, CIS documents, income proof and summaries in one place.
  • WhatsApp receipt intake is now live, with email intake available too.
  • Read-only accountant portal and PDF/CSV packs.
  • Digital records and quarter-ready summaries for your accountant's MTD workflow.
  • No bloated accounting suite: TidGo focuses on receipts, income proof, tidy summaries and accountant handoff.
  • No business bank account or bank connection required.
  • Free during early access.

TidGo helps with simple Making Tax Digital record preparation. It does not submit tax returns to HMRC and does not replace professional tax or accounting advice.
